1. Quick Start

The tool runs as a local web application on your computer. No data is sent to external servers apart from API calls to Anthropic for the AI analysis.

Requirements

  • Node.js (version 18 or later)
  • Anthropic API key for AI features (optional but recommended)
  • Modern web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox)

Installation

  1. Open Terminal on your Mac
  2. Navigate to the project folder: cd ~/Desktop/Nav/lulea-decision-tool
  3. Install dependencies (only needed the first time): npm install
  4. Enter your API key — open server.js and paste the key on line 6: const API_KEY = 'sk-ant-your-key-here';
  5. Start the server: node server.js
  6. Open http://localhost:8080 in your browser
Tip
The tool works without an API key — the decision tree, scores, profiles and comparisons are fully available. However, the AI chat requires a valid key.

3. Decision Tree

The decision tree is the core of the tool. It models the entire procurement decision in a hierarchical structure with four evaluation pillars and over 40 detailed nodes.

Evaluation pillars and weights

PillarWeightDescription
Operational Capability 35% Air defence/IAMD, anti-submarine warfare, surface strike, naval command, endurance, future-proofing
Delivery & Schedule Risk 30% Design maturity, schedule credibility, integration risk, programme management
Life-cycle Cost 20% Acquisition cost, through-life maintenance, manning & training
Industry & Supply Security 15% Swedish industrial participation, sovereignty, strategic partnership value

Navigation

  1. Click a node in the left panel to expand/collapse it and display details in the right panel
  2. The right panel shows: breadcrumb navigation, node description, influence vectors (colour-coded by impact level) and score cards per company
  3. Each score card shows the company name, score (1–10) and an edit button. Click the card header to expand/collapse details (bar, rationale, history and action buttons). Cards are expanded by default.

Score card buttons

ButtonFunctionUse
Improve Score AI suggests concrete actions to raise the score Identify improvement areas and action plans
Suggest Actions AI recommends concrete steps the company can take Diplomacy, technology demonstration, partnerships
Challenge AI debates the score with arguments for and against Stress-test the assessment and question assumptions

Influence vectors

Each node can have associated influence vectors — factors that affect the outcome. These are displayed as colour-coded tags:

  • High — Strong, documented impact on the decision
  • Medium — Noticeable impact, less decisive
  • Low — Limited direct impact

3b. Score Editing

All scores in the decision tree can be edited directly. Changes are saved to the server and propagate automatically to all views — Comparison, Profiles and Scenarios.

Editing a score

  1. Click the edit icon (pencil) in the score card header. A modal opens.
  2. Drag the slider to adjust the score (1.0–10.0). An impact preview shows how the change affects category and overall scores.
  3. Optionally enter a new rationale — existing text is kept if the field is left empty.
  4. Enter a mandatory change motivation explaining why the score is being adjusted. This is logged in the history.
  5. Click Save. The change is applied immediately across all views and persisted on the server.
Automatic propagation
When a score is edited, weighted averages are recalculated in real time. Aggregate scores on category nodes, the comparison table, company profiles and scenarios all update immediately.

Change history

Each score card displays its change history directly below the rationale (expand the card to see it). The history shows:

  • Old and new score with delta (e.g. 7.0 → 8.5 (+1.5))
  • Timestamp of the change
  • The motivation provided at the time of the edit

A yellow edited badge appears in the score card header for scores that have been modified.

Deleting individual changes

Click the × button next to a history entry to delete that change. The page reloads and all remaining changes are re-applied. A full change log is also available under the Change Log tab in the navigation bar.

Reset all changes

Click "Reset All" in the change log to delete all edits and revert to the original scores.

Server storage
All score changes are saved on the server (not in the browser's localStorage). Data is preserved between sessions and browsers as long as the server is running.

5. Comparison View

The comparison view displays a complete table with all three candidates' scores side by side:

  • Green dot (●) marks the leading candidate per criterion
  • Scores are colour-coded: green (≥8), yellow (6–7.9), red (<6)
  • Category rows show weighted averages
  • Click a row to navigate directly to that node in the decision tree
Quick interpretation
Look for patterns: Where does a candidate have consistently green scores? Where are there red scores that could be decisive? The comparison view gives the fastest overview of the competitive landscape.

6. Scenarios

The scenarios view combines custom scenario projections with four predefined outcomes based on open-source analysis.

Custom scenario projection

At the top of the view is an input area where you can describe any hypothetical scenario. The AI (Claude Sonnet) analyses how the scenario would change scores across all 53 leaf criteria and all three companies.

  1. Type a scenario description in the text field, e.g. "Sweden decides crew size is the absolute top priority" or "Babcock/Saab demonstrates Aster 30 integration on Arrowhead 120".
  2. Click "Analyze Scenario". A loading indicator appears while the AI analyses.
  3. The results panel shows a list of suggested score changes. Each row displays:
    • Criterion name and path in the decision tree
    • Company and current score → suggested score
    • Confidence level (high, medium, low)
    • Rationale from the AI
  4. Apply — apply an individual score change (saved in the change log with prefix [Scenario: ...]).
  5. Apply All — apply all not-yet-applied suggestions at once.
  6. Clear — clear the results and reset the input.
Selective application
You do not need to apply all suggestions. Review each row and apply only the changes you judge to be reasonable. Applied changes propagate immediately to all views (Decision Tree, Profiles, Comparison).
AI model
The scenario projection uses Claude Sonnet (not Opus) for the best balance of speed and quality when analysing all 53 criteria. Requires a valid API key.

Predefined scenarios

Below the input area, four scenario cards based on open-source analysis are displayed:

ScenarioDescription

7. AI Analysis (Claude Opus 4.6)

The AI panel opens as a side panel from the right. It is powered by Claude Opus 4.6 — Anthropic's most capable model — with specialised knowledge of Swedish defence procurement and the Luleå programme.

Opening the AI panel

  • Click Improve Score, Suggest Actions or Challenge on a score card
  • Click "Deep Analysis" or "Win Strategy" on aggregate categories
  • Click "Explore with AI" on any node
  • Click "Explore Scenario with AI" on the scenarios view

Quick actions

The AI panel shows context-adapted suggested questions as round buttons above the text field. Click one to immediately submit the question. You can also type your own questions freely in the text field.

Tips for effective AI usage

Be specific
"How can Naval Group reduce the integration risk for 9LV CMS on the FDI platform?" yields better answers than "How can they improve?"
Challenge actively
Use the "Challenge" button to stress-test assessments. Ask the AI to argue for both higher AND lower scores.
Follow-up questions
The AI remembers the conversation context. Ask follow-up questions to go deeper: "Elaborate on point 3" or "What risks do you see with that proposal?"
Compare perspectives
Open the AI panel for the same criterion from different companies' perspectives to see how the arguments differ.

8. Guide for Company Representatives

Below is how a representative from one of the competing companies can use the tool strategically.

A. Understand your position

  1. Go to Company Profiles and select your company. Study the overall score and score per pillar.
  2. Identify your three strongest and three weakest criteria in the score table.
  3. Go to the Comparison View and see where you lead (green dot) vs. where you trail.

B. Identify improvement opportunities

  1. Navigate to your weakest nodes in the decision tree (red/yellow scores).
  2. Click Improve Score to get AI-generated improvement suggestions.
  3. Click Suggest Actions for concrete steps you can take in the next 6 months.
  4. Focus on nodes with high weight — an improvement from 6 to 8 on a 5%-node has more impact than the same improvement on a 1%-node.
Prioritisation rule
Impact = (score improvement) × (node weight). Focus on highly weighted nodes where you score low — that is the fastest way to improve your overall score.

C. Understand your competitors

  1. Study your competitors' profiles — their strengths show where they will argue most forcefully to FMV.
  2. Their weaknesses are potential attack points in your own positioning.
  3. Use the AI's "Win Strategy" function at category level to get strategic recommendations.

D. Test and challenge assessments

  1. Use Challenge on scores you believe are incorrect — the AI presents arguments both ways.
  2. Document your counter-arguments and evidence for internal presentations.
  3. Test your narratives: Ask "What is the strongest argument against [your company] on this point?" to prepare for FMV's critical review.

E. Scenario planning

  1. Go to Scenarios and explore the scenario where your company wins — what conditions are required?
  2. Explore scenarios where a competitor wins — what can you do to prevent it?
  3. Use the custom scenario projection to test specific hypotheses, e.g. "FMV doubles the weight on manning" or "Navantia partners with Saab for CMS integration". Review the AI's suggestions and apply selectively.
  4. Use the AI to identify signals to watch that indicate which direction the decision is heading.

F. Strategic action plan

Recommended workflow
  1. Map your position (Profiles + Comparison) — 30 min
  2. Prioritise 5 key criteria with the greatest impact potential — 15 min
  3. Run AI analysis on each key criterion (Improve + Actions) — 45 min
  4. Stress-test your assumptions (Challenge on 3–5 critical scores) — 30 min
  5. Compile action plan and narrative — 30 min

Data sources: All scores and analyses are based on publicly available sources — FMV press releases, Swedish Parliament propositions, Swedish Armed Forces budget submissions, Swedish business press (SVT, Affärsvärlden), Reuters, suppliers' public communications, and defence analytical publications. The tool does not replace professional advice.

Privacy: The application runs locally. Score changes are saved on the server in memory (no external database). API calls to Anthropic contain only the current query and context — no personal data or classified information should be entered.
